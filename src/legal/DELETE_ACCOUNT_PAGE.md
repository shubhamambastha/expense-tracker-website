# Delete your Jebbo account

> **Before publishing:** host this at `https://jebbo.imcremars.xyz/delete-account` (the URL given in Play Console → Data safety, see [APP_PRIVACY_LABELS.md](./APP_PRIVACY_LABELS.md#google-play-data-safety)). Today that path falls back to the landing page, which Google treats as no deletion URL. Fill in every `[BRACKETED]` value with the same answer as [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md). Google requires that this page names the app (Jebbo) and the developer as they appear on the store listing, and lets people ask for deletion without reinstalling the app.

You can delete your Jebbo account, and all of the data in it, whenever you want.

## Option 1: in the app (fastest)

1. Open Jebbo and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Account**.
3. Type **DELETE** and tap **Delete forever**.

Your account is deleted right away, and you're signed out.

## Option 2: without the app

If you've uninstalled Jebbo or can't sign in, email **support@imcremars.xyz** from the address you use to sign in, with the subject **"Delete my Jebbo account"**. We'll confirm that the request comes from the account owner, delete the account within **30 days** (usually much sooner), and reply once it's done.

## What gets deleted

- Your login (email, password hash, and any Google or Apple sign-in link)
- Your name, phone number, and profile settings
- Every transaction, account, budget, category, recurring payment, and saved payer or payee

## What we keep, and for how long

- **Database backups:** none. We don't keep database backups, so no backup copy remains.
- **Service logs:** request logs hold your account ID, the endpoint, and the response status and timing, but no financial data. They're deleted after **7 days**.

We keep nothing else once your account is deleted.

## Guest mode

If you used Jebbo without an account, your data never reached us, so there's nothing for us to delete. It stays on your device until you delete the app.

## Just want a fresh start?

**Settings → Data & Privacy → Reset My Account** deletes all of your records but keeps your login.
